Prompt 1: 
What are the core functionalities or features of our application. What it does or provides.
In my point of view it is:
* core idea of upload pdf or create article and sell or buy online.
* session: 1:1 interaction.
* Analytics.
* Notification and follow feature.
* QnA, Reviews or feedback, Community.
* Any others. List all.


Answer: 
At a high level, LearnMint provides these core functionalities:
1. Knowledge Publishing – Upload PDF resources or write and publish articles.
2. Knowledge Marketplace – Discover, browse, buy, and sell educational resources.
3. Content Monetization – Earn revenue by selling digital learning content.
4. Content Consumption – Read purchased PDFs and articles in an integrated viewer.
5. Creator Workspace – Manage resources, drafts, earnings, and analytics.
6. Learner Workspace – Access purchased resources, bookmarks, and learning history.
7. 1:1 Mentorship / Live Sessions – Book and manage one-on-one sessions with creators.
8. Community & Engagement – Q&A, reviews, ratings, comments, and creator announcements.
9. Social Features – Follow creators and receive updates from them.
10. Notifications – Alerts for purchases, new content, sessions, announcements, and activities.
11. Analytics & Insights – Performance insights for creators and platform administrators.
12. Administration & Moderation – Manage users, resources, transactions, reports, and platform settings.
13. Authentication & User Management – Secure login, registration, profile management, and role-based access.

                     * Resources

⭐ Nice to Have (If Time Permits)
These make the platform richer but are not essential for a working product.
                     * Follow Creator
                     * Notifications

                     * Community Hub

Prompt 3: 
Okay which one feature from nice to have features list I can pick that stand out my project?
Answer: 
If your goal is to stand out during CDAC evaluation and placements, I would choose a feature that is:
---
My ranking:-
🥇 1. 1:1 Mentorship / Live Session Booking ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Best Choice)


This is the feature I'd choose.


Why?
                     * Very few student projects include it.
                     * It perfectly fits LearnMint's vision.
                     * You don't need to build video calling yourself.


You can implement:
                     * Creator sets available slots.
                     * Learner books a slot.
                     * Creator accepts/rejects.
                     * Session appears in both dashboards.
                     * Store a Google Meet/Zoom link (or dummy link for demo).


This showcases:
                     * Scheduling
                     * Booking system
                     * Notifications
                     * Role-based workflows
                     * Database relationships


---
🥈 2. Follow Creator + Notifications ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆


A very realistic social feature.


Flow:
Follow Creator
        ↓
Creator publishes new resource
        ↓
Followers receive notification


This makes the platform feel alive with relatively low implementation effort.


---
🥉 3. Reviews + Q&A ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆


After purchasing a resource:
                     * Ask questions
                     * Leave reviews
                     * Rate the resource
This is useful but quite common.


---
4. Reading Progress + Bookmarks ⭐⭐⭐☆


Nice user experience improvement, but it doesn't make the project stand out as much.


---
My recommendation:


If you can add only one extra feature:


> Choose 1:1 Mentorship / Live Session Booking.
It transforms LearnHub from just a content marketplace into a knowledge platform, because creators don't only sell PDFs and articles—they can also offer personalized guidance.


It also gives you an excellent story to explain in interviews:
> "LearnMint enables creators to monetize their expertise in two ways: by selling digital resources (PDFs and articles) and by offering paid one-on-one mentorship sessions."


That's a much stronger value proposition than simply saying, "Users can upload PDFs and buy them.”
